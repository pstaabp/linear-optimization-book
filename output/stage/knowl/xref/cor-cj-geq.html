Corollary 6.2.5.

From above, show that
🔗
\begin{equation} c_j = \sum_{i=1}^m a_{ij} y^{\star}_i - y^{\star}_{m+j}\tag{6.2.5} \end{equation}
🔗
and then conclude that
🔗
\begin{equation} \sum_{i=1}^m a_{ij} y^{\star}_i \geq c_j.\tag{6.2.6} \end{equation}
🔗
🔗

Proof.

Equating coefficients of Corollary 6.2.4, leads to (6.2.5). Since both \(y^{\star}_i \geq 0\) and \(y^{\star}_{m+j} \geq 0\) from Lemma 6.2.1, this leads to (6.2.6).
