Algorithm 1.3.19. Fraction Free Pivot.

If the matrix \(A\) has only integer entries, then the following will perform a fraction-free pivot at row \(i\text{,}\) column \(j\text{,}\) which will eliminate a column, but not produce a 1 at the pivot point. The result will be a matrix with all integer entries.
🔗
Let \(a_{i,j}\) be the coefficient of a \(m \times n\) matrix (and an integer). Then
🔗
\begin{equation*} -a_{k,j} R_i + a_{i,j} R_k \to R_k \qquad \text{for $k=1, \ldots m$, $k \neq i$} \end{equation*}
🔗
will eliminate column \(j\text{.}\) If in addition, the pivot is desired to be positive, then also apply the step
🔗
\begin{equation*} - R_i \to R_i \end{equation*}
🔗
Lastly, let \(d\) be the positive integer that is the \((i,j)\) entry. The other rows will be multiples of \(d\) and the row operation
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{1}{d} R_k \to R_k \qquad \text{for $i \neq k$} \end{equation*}
🔗
and any other pivots will now have a entry of \(d\text{.}\)
